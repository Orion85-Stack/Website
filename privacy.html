Privacy Policy

How Praxis Insights collects, uses, protects, and manages personal information.

Last updated: May 2026

1. Introduction

Praxis Insights respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and protect personal information when you visit our website, download our resources, or contact us about our services.

This policy is intended to align with the principles of the Protection of Personal Information Act, 4 of 2013, commonly known as POPIA.

2. Who We Are

Praxis Insights provides data analytics, business intelligence, workflow optimisation, project delivery, and dashboard consulting services.

For the purposes of this policy, Praxis Insights is the responsible party for personal information collected through this website.

3. Personal Information We Collect

We may collect the following personal information when you complete a form on our website:

  • Your full name
  • Your email address
  • Your service interest
  • Your message or enquiry details
  • The source of your enquiry, such as “Dashboard Checklist” or “Contact Form”

We do not intentionally collect special personal information, such as health information, religious beliefs, political views, biometric information, or information about children.

4. How We Collect Personal Information

We collect personal information directly from you when you:

  • Submit the contact form
  • Download the Dashboard Audit Checklist
  • Request a discovery call or data audit
  • Email us directly

Our website forms are processed through Formspree, a third-party form submission service. Formspree receives the information you submit and sends it to us by email.

5. Why We Use Your Personal Information

We use your personal information for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your enquiry
  • To provide the resource or checklist you requested
  • To schedule or discuss a discovery call
  • To understand your business needs
  • To provide information about relevant Praxis Insights services
  • To improve our website, forms, and client communication process

We only process personal information where we have a lawful reason to do so, such as your consent, your request for information, a legitimate business purpose, or the need to take steps before entering into a possible business relationship.

6. Direct Marketing

If you submit your details through our website, we may follow up with you about the specific resource, enquiry, or service you requested.

We will not sell your personal information. If we send you marketing or follow-up communication, you may ask us to stop at any time by contacting us at: hello@praxisinsights.co.za.

7. Sharing Your Personal Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited personal information with trusted service providers only where necessary to operate this website and respond to enquiries.

These providers may include:

  • Formspree, for form processing
  • GitHub Pages, for website hosting
  • Email service providers, for communication

These third-party providers may process information in other countries. Where this happens, we aim to use reputable providers that apply appropriate security and privacy safeguards.

8. How We Protect Your Information

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against loss, unauthorised access, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

These measures may include:

  • Using HTTPS through GitHub Pages
  • Using trusted third-party form processing
  • Limiting access to enquiry emails
  • Avoiding collection of unnecessary personal information
  • Using basic website security controls such as a Content Security Policy

No online transmission or storage system is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect the information you provide.

9. How Long We Keep Your Information

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, unless a longer retention period is required or allowed by law.

Enquiry information may be retained for follow-up, business administration, record-keeping, and client relationship purposes.

10. Your Rights

Under POPIA, you have rights in relation to your personal information. These may include the right to:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your personal information
  • Object to certain processing of your personal information
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • Request that we stop sending direct marketing communication
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa

To exercise your rights, contact us at: hello@praxisinsights.co.za.

11. Cookies and Tracking

At this stage, our website does not intentionally use analytics cookies, advertising pixels, or behavioural tracking tools.

If we add analytics or advertising tools in future, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain what is collected, why it is collected, and how you can manage your preferences.

12. Links to Other Websites

Our website may include links to external websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, security, or content of external websites.
